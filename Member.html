How posting works

1. Select a package and transfer the package fee to the displayed bank account.

2. Upload the transfer slip from the Package & Payment page.

3. The administrator reviews the payment and assigns the selected package.

4. Your posting balance is counted automatically. Posting stops when the limit is reached or the package expires.
